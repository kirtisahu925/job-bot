Fairsheet: profile and settings

Stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local). Nothing here is sent anywhere except your own Supabase project when you sign in, look up a job or save one.

1. Connect your tracker

2. Your application profile

3. Answers to recurring questions

One per line: keywords => answer. A field whose question contains all the keywords gets the answer. Used for yes/no radios too.

4. Default resume

Attached when a job has no tailored resume yet.

5. Apply queue

From the popup, the queue opens your ready jobs one at a time in background tabs: a tailored resume, ATS and match at or above the minimums below. It fills each application, attaches that job's resume, steps through Next pages, and stops at the Submit button, outlined in green. It never submits. You review each tab, press Submit yourself, then Mark applied. It also stops early at any question your profile and saved answers don't cover, such as free-text answers, consent boxes, sign-ups and skill-specific experience.

LinkedIn's User Agreement forbids automated use of its site, and LinkedIn can restrict accounts it thinks are automated. Leave this off unless you accept that risk, and keep the daily number low.
